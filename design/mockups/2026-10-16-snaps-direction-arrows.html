Snaps: orientation in two steps

Step 1, Make the board match the photo: Rotate board and Swap colours, drawing only. When it looks right, press Board looks right. Step 2, Make the game right: who you're analysing for, which corner of the board as drawn is that colour's home board, and Mirror checkers. Hovering or focusing a number 1–4 washes that quadrant of the big board in the focus blue. The chosen home board gets a rail on the board's frame, outside the playing area, so it never covers a checker or meets a move arrow. On touch, a tap selects the number and focuses it, so the wash shows the selected quadrant. Every frame works on its own. The 1280 step 2 frames open with a hover preview on corner 2.
