A · Clubroom Recommended

A quiet club library: warm ivory paper, ink-black type, a walnut and bone board. A serif for titles and big numbers, a clean sans for everything you scan. Colour only where it means something.

B · Quiet Ink

Swiss and precise, like a good analytics tool: cool neutrals, one typeface family, hairlines, no decoration. The board turns graphite and grey. It looks premium through restraint.

C · Midnight Felt

Casino-salon glamour, built dark-first: green baize, brass accents, oxblood points, a high-contrast display serif. The most dramatic of the three, and the one that fights the severity colours most.
